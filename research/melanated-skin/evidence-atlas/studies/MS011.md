# MS011 · Topical prevention from high energy visible light-induced pigmentation by 2-mercaptonicotinoyl glycine, but not by ascorbic acid antioxidant: 2 randomized controlled trials

**Citation:** Piffaut et al. (2025). *Frontiers in Pharmacology.*  
**Primary source:** [PubMed PMID 41142247](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41142247/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2025.1651068) · [Open-access full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12546040/)  
**Evidence classification:** Active treatments within III–IV phototypes  
**Source check:** PubMed and open-access article checked (reviewed 2026-11-25)

## Study at a glance

| Dimension | Details |
|---|---|
| Intervention(s) | Ascorbic acid 7%; 2-mercaptonicotinoyl glycine (2-MNG) 0.5% and 1% |
| Research theme | Visible light and sunscreen |
| Study type | Two randomized controlled trials |
| Population | 58 Fitzpatrick III–IV individuals across two studies |
| Phototypes | III–IV |
| Reported sample | 58 total |
| Exposure / follow-up | Experimental high-energy visible light protocol |
| Endpoints | Prevention of visible-light-induced pigmentation |

## What the paper found

2-MNG reduced visible-light-induced pigmentation under trial conditions; topical 7% ascorbic acid did not demonstrate the same preventive outcome relative to control.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Match a proposed antioxidant claim to the causal stimulus and endpoint; UV-related and visible-light-related pigmentation are not interchangeable.

**What this study cannot establish:** Does not test treatment of existing spots or establish failure of vitamin C for other uses; limited to phototypes III–IV and study vehicles.

**Appraisal flags:** Specific light source and endpoint; cannot extrapolate to general vitamin C efficacy.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
