## What the paper found

Acne efficacy was broadly similar across groups. Published analysis reported fewer moderate-to-severe dryness events among Black patients; specific percentages should be checked in the full paper before reuse.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Separately analyse efficacy and tolerability; neither can be inferred from pigmentation alone.

**What this study cannot establish:** Pooled subgroup analysis; formulations and treatment regimens specific to adapalene 0.1% gel; population labels are not phototype readings.

**Appraisal flags:** Meta-analysis subgroup; do not generalize to all retinoids.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
