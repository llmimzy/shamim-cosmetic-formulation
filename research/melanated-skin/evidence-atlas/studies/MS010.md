## What the paper found

Both routines improved appearance over baseline; at 12 weeks, 36% of melasma participants in the SPF 50 + iron-oxide group met the reported superior improvement in L* versus 0% in SPF 50 alone.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Compare broad-spectrum UV protection alone versus combined UV and visible-light photoprotection for pigmentation outcomes.

**What this study cannot establish:** Product/regimen rather than isolated ingredient comparison; the 36% figure applies to a particular study endpoint and subgroup, not a general cure rate.

**Appraisal flags:** Regimen study; specific subgroup endpoint.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
