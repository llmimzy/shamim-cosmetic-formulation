## What the paper found

Human treatment produced a significant or slight improvement in skin lightness/lentigines in light- and dark-skinned groups respectively, as phrased in the abstract. The exact subgroup statistical comparison requires full-text verification.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Apparent variation in response supports testing the same inhibitor across measured melanin levels, rather than extrapolating animal activity.

**What this study cannot establish:** Small subgroup sizes have been reported secondarily; primary abstract gives no subgroup denominators, and a formal between-group effect estimate was not confirmed.

**Appraisal flags:** Preclinical data; no confirmed interaction test; possible subgroup imprecision.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
