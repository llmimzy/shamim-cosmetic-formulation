# MS008 · Sodium-lauryl-sulphate-induced cutaneous irritation. Comparison of white and Hispanic subjects

**Citation:** Berardesca & Maibach (1988). *Contact Dermatitis.*

## What the paper found

TEWL and water-content responses were greater in Hispanic participants, while measured microcirculatory responses were similar between groups.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Separate barrier response from vascular response when measuring irritation, and avoid ethnicity-based blanket claims.

**What this study cannot establish:** Hispanic is an ethnic category covering many pigment levels; small historical irritant study.

**Appraisal flags:** Ethnicity as proxy; short irritation challenge.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
