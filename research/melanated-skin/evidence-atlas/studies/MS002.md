# MS002 · Racial differences in sodium lauryl sulphate induced cutaneous irritation: black and white

**Citation:** Berardesca & Maibach (1988). *Contact Dermatitis.*

## What the paper found

SLS at 0.5% and 2% produced differences in barrier and erythematous responses depending on skin group and pretreatment site; the pattern was not a simple universal increase in sensitivity in one group.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Evaluate cleanser exposure and irritation using both barrier measurements and clinical signs in a diverse sample.

**What this study cannot establish:** Very small all-male sample; group labels are not quantitative melanin measurement; patch test is not a finished-product use trial.

**Appraisal flags:** Small sample; race as proxy; irritation challenge is not cosmetic efficacy.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
