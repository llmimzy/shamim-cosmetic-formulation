# MS004 · Differential regulation of tyrosinase activity in skin of white and black individuals in vivo by topical retinoic acid

## What the paper found

Short exposure increased measured tyrosinase activity in White skin but not Black skin under the reported experimental conditions.

## Interpretation for formulation research

The same retinoid can yield different short-term biochemical responses across populations; distinguish mechanistic markers from clinical pigmentation outcomes.

**What this study cannot establish:** Short-term mechanistic response, not a long-term hyperpigmentation efficacy comparison; no basis to conclude tretinoin is ineffective in darker skin.

**Appraisal flags:** Biochemical surrogate endpoint; not clinical efficacy.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
