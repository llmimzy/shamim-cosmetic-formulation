## What the paper found

Tolerability patterns differed by recorded race/ethnicity; Black patients had lower adjusted odds of intolerance than Asian patients in this cohort.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Design prospective tolerability assessments with carefully measured skin pigmentation, treatment exposures and follow-up.

**What this study cannot establish:** Single-centre retrospective medical records; 21.4% lost to follow-up as reported in full text; residual confounding; race not melanin index.

**Appraisal flags:** Observational; selection and reporting bias; no causal attribution.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
