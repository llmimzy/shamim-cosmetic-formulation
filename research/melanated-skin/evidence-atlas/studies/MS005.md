# MS005 · Effects of glycolic acid on light-induced skin pigmentation in Asian and Caucasian subjects

## What the paper found

The investigation reported altered tanning responses following glycolic acid pretreatment, rather than proof that glycolic acid uniformly prevents pigmentation.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Exfoliation studies should include UV-exposure context, irritation and pigmentation follow-up when targeting PIH-prone users.

**What this study cannot establish:** Small study; phototype overlap and ethnic group labels confound a pigmentation-specific explanation; no generalized clinical recommendation.

**Appraisal flags:** Small sample; exposure paradigm; phototype/ethnicity overlap.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
