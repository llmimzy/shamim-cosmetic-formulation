# MS013 · Effective reduction of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation with the tyrosinase inhibitor isobutylamido-thiazolyl-resorcinol (Thiamidol)

**Citation:** Roggenkamp et al. (2021). *International Journal of Cosmetic Science.*  
**Primary source:** [PubMed PMID 33559186](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33559186/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/ics.12694) · [Open-access full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8251899/)  
**Evidence classification:** Treatment vs vehicle within darker phototypes  
**Source check:** PubMed abstract and disclosure checked (reviewed 2026-11-25)

## Study at a glance

| Dimension | Details |
|---|---|
| Intervention(s) | Isobutylamido thiazolyl resorcinol (Thiamidol) |
| Research theme | Pigmentation |
| Study type | Controlled experiment and clinical studies |
| Population | Dark-skinned volunteers with experimentally induced or acne-associated PIH |
| Phototypes | V and VI cohorts reported in full text |
| Reported sample | Several studies; subgroup sizes vary |
| Exposure / follow-up | Approximately 12 weeks |
| Endpoints | PIH lesion visibility, melanin index and clinical grading |

## What the paper found

Thiamidol-containing products improved suction-blister and acne-related PIH endpoints over vehicle or baseline under the study conditions.

## Interpretation for formulation research

Distinguish improvement in existing PIH from prevention of newly induced PIH; track both lesion and surrounding-skin colour.

**What this study cannot establish:** Several authors employed by the product manufacturer; patented ingredient; selected formulations and phototypes, not a universal comparative response.

**Appraisal flags:** Industry conflicts of interest; multiple different sub-studies.

## Follow-up research question

How would the outcome change after controlling for melanin index, the actual finished-product vehicle, exposure, baseline barrier function, pigmentation endpoints and a pre-specified between-group contrast where appropriate?
