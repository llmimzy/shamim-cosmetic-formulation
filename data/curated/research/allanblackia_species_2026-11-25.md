# Allanblackia species check (2026-11-25)

**Question:** ING-129 is recorded as "Allanblackia Floribunda Seed Butter". Is that a valid INCI name?

**Finding:** No CosIng entry for *A. floribunda* seed butter was found. CosIng lists two Allanblackia seed butters:

| INCI name | CosIng ID | Species origin | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALLANBLACKIA STUHLMANNII SEED BUTTER | 94420 | Tanzania (Eastern Arc mountains); currently the main commercial source | skinsort.com (CosIng data); cosmileeurope.eu; Wikipedia "Allanblackia oil"; Lush ingredient page |
| ALLANBLACKIA PARVIFLORA SEED BUTTER | 98136 | Upper Guinea, Ghana westwards | skinsort.com (CosIng data); cosingchecker.com |

*A. floribunda* is a real species of commercial interest (Nigeria to DRC; PMC4613311), but its butter has no INCI name in the sources checked. No CAS number was found for either listed butter.

**Note on taxonomy:** recent treatments move *Allanblackia* into *Garcinia* (*A. stuhlmannii* = *Garcinia stuhlmannii*). INCI names still use *Allanblackia*.

**Recommendation (owner decision needed):** change ING-129 to **Allanblackia Stuhlmannii Seed Butter**. It is East African, it is the species actually traded, and it has a valid INCI name. Until the owner decides, the record keeps its original name and stays `unresolved`.

**Absence caveat:** "not found" means not found in the sources above. CosIng itself was not queried directly, because the session's network policy blocks it.
